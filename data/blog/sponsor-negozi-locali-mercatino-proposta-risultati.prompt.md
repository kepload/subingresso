# Copertina originale IA

Strumento: image_gen.imagegen integrato. Una generazione originale, nessuna immagine di riferimento. Scena inventata, persone e attività non reali. Nessun consenso, evento o rapporto commerciale rappresentato come osservato.

## Prompt effettivo

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: editorial blog cover, landscape 16:9, intended final export 1600x900.
Primary request: an invented scene about a small Italian neighborhood market organizer discussing a modest local sponsorship with a stationery shop owner.
Scene: outside a small generic stationery and printing shop opening onto an Italian town square; a few modest craft market stalls softly visible in the background.
Subject: two fictional adults around a small practical table, reviewing an unbranded printed event poster with blank geometric spaces for sponsor logos, a clipboard and a short stack of freshly printed leaflets. Natural hands and everyday clothes. They are looking at the materials, no handshake.
Style: natural editorial photography, believable materials, restrained colors, daylight, documentary composition, wide framing at eye level.
Constraints: no real people, no recognizable brands, no readable text, no numbers, no watermarks, no fake UI, no luxury atmosphere, no food or restaurant setting. Sponsorship is suggested by reviewing print materials, not by crowds or money. Everything in the image is fictional.
